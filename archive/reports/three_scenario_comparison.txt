================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

FULFILLMENT CHAMPION:
  - Anticipated: Wins Downtown Crush by 9.5 points (66.8% vs 57.3%)
  - Network: Wins Popup Problem by 3.2 points (69.5% vs 66.3%)
  - Algorithms with bundling consistently outperform single-order strategies

SPEED CHAMPION:
  - Hungarian: Fastest avg delivery time in ALL scenarios (21.0, 25.0, 25.7 min)
  - Hungarian: Fastest P90 delivery time in ALL scenarios (26.1, 31.6, 32.9 min)
  - Speed comes at cost of fulfillment volume

EFFICIENCY CHAMPION:
  - Anticipated: Best distance efficiency in both complete scenarios
    * Downtown: 383.8 km (16% less than 2nd place)
    * Popup: 268.3 km (13% less than 2nd place)
  - Bundling reduces total distance traveled by 15-21%

BUNDLING POWER:
  - Anticipated achieves 2.21-2.68 orders per bundle
  - Network achieves 1.56-1.76 orders per bundle
  - Simple achieves 1.11-1.43 orders per bundle
  - Greedy/Hungarian: 1.00 (no bundling)

THE HUNGARIAN PARADOX:
  - Fastest individual deliveries but NOT highest fulfillment
  - Optimizes for speed over volume
  - Best for customer experience, suboptimal for throughput

SCENARIO-SPECIFIC PATTERNS:
  - Downtown Crush: Anticipated dominates (+16% fulfillment)
  - Popup Problem: Network slightly edges Anticipated (+3% fulfillment)
